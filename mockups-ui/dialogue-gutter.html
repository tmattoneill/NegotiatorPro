How should I open the price conversation if they haven't put a number forward yet?

Don't be the first to anchor unless you have strong information. Open with a calibrated question that surfaces their constraints. Ask what their budget allows, then let the silence stretch.
Voss · ch. 7 Fisher & Ury · p. 84

They mentioned budget constraints twice. Should I probe?

Yes. Mirror the phrase, then label the emotion. "Budget constraints" — let the silence do the work. They'll often expand on what they meant without being asked.
Voss · mirroring

What if they push back on the timeline?

Don't bargain on the timeline directly. Reframe — ask why that date matters. The reason behind it is the real lever.
Ury · ch. 2
